Encrypted Ballot Board

📌 Public Transparency: All cast ballots are encrypted and displayed here. Voters can verify their ballot inclusion using their tracker ID.

How It Works

Ballot Integrity: Each ballot is encrypted with RSA-OAEP and assigned a unique tracker ID. You can verify your ballot appears on this board after casting your vote. Tracker IDs are SHA-256 hashes of encrypted ballot content.
Privacy Protection: Ballots remain encrypted on the public board. Only administrators with the private key can decrypt and tally results after the election closes.
Single Vote Guarantee: Each voter credential can only be spent once, preventing duplicate voting.
